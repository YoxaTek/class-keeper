## Brand & Style

This design system pairs the scholarly rigor of higher education with the grounded warmth of architectural ceramics and earthen minerals. Built specifically for academic researchers, educators, and curriculum directors operating in high-focus environments, the interface rejects cold, clinical blue-tinted dark themes in favor of charcoal, kiln-fired terracotta, and soft ivory parchment.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Academic Elegance** with **Tactile Warmth**:
- **Quiet Depth:** Surfaces evoke rich slate chalkboards, fired clay pottery, and leather-bound archives.
- **Cognitive Ease:** Reading long-form research, syllabus builders, and grading interfaces requires high legibility without the eye fatigue of stark white-on-pure-black contrast.
- **Disciplined Precision:** Clean geometric alignments and structured modularity prevent information-dense academic workflows from feeling cluttered.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an earthen dark mode architecture, utilizing amber-skewed charcoals and kiln-burnt terracotta to provide unmistakable hierarchy while preserving optical calm.

### Surface Hierarchy (Clay & Charcoal)
- **Base Canvas (`#121110`):** Ultra-deep umber-charcoal background providing an immersive, low-glare foundation.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#1a1816`):** Primary panel, sidebar, and workspace tone; subtle warmth prevents clinical starkness.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#24211e`):** Card containers, grouped lists, and contextual toolbars.
- **Surface Level 3 (`#2e2a26`):** Floating dialogs, elevated popovers, and hovered states.
- **Border / Outline (`#3f3a34`):** Low-contrast structural perimeter strokes preserving visual containment.

### Accent Spectrum (Terracotta & Flame)
- **Primary Terracotta (`#ea580c`):** High-priority actions, primary active states, and dominant focal points.
- **Vibrant Terracotta (`#f97316`):** Interactive hover states, badge accents, and highlight callouts.
- **Warm Apricot Clay (`#fb923c`):** Tertiary accents, tag borders, chart highlights, and focus rings.

### Text & Glyphs (Parchment & Ivory)
- **High-Emphasis Text (`#f5f3f0`):** Soft ivory, delivering maximum legibility without retina burn.
- **Medium-Emphasis Text (`#e2ded9`):** Warm parchment for secondary metadata, descriptions, and labels.
- **Muted Text / Icons (`#a8a29e`):** Clay stone for disabled states, timestamps, placeholders, and inactive icons.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles. Its geometric cleanliness, open counters, and humanist proportions make it ideally suited for dense academic dashboards, rubrics, research publications, and gradebooks.

- **Headlines:** Display and headline weights carry subtle negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to create commanding, authoritative titles without crowding.
- **Body:** Body text prioritizes comfortable, breathable vertical metrics (26px line height on 16px body) ensuring sustained comprehension during long reading and editorial sessions.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Labels leverage medium and semibold weights with positive tracking (`0.01em` to `0.04em`) to maintain legibility at micro scales across chips, course codes, and status tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by a structured 12-column fluid grid, balanced by a baseline 8-point rhythm (subdivided into 4-point increments for fine component details).

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid with `gutter: 1.5rem` and outer canvas `margin: 2rem` (expanding to `3rem` on ultra-wide screens). Sidebars for course navigation and lecture trees maintain a fixed 280px width, while main analytical and document views span the remaining grid.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout. Structural sidebars collapse into slide-out drawers or narrow icon rails. Gutters remain `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column flow with `gutter-mobile: 1rem` and edge padding `margin-mobile: 1rem`. Multi-column analytical widgets stack vertically into single full-width cards.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid jarring, muddy drops in a dark workspace, elevation relies primarily on **tonal surface stacking** reinforced by **warm earthen ambient glows** and **subtle low-contrast outlines**.

1. **Surface Stacking:** Structural elevation starts at `#121110` (ground canvas), ascending through `#1a1816` (content panels), `#24211e` (cards), up to `#2e2a26` (floating dialogs and menus).
2. **Subtle Containment:** Elevated elements feature a 1px perimeter border tinted in warm clay (`#3f3a34` or `#4a433b`), defining boundaries cleanly without harsh delineation.
3. **Ambient Warm Glows:** Overlays and floating modals utilize soft, extra-diffused drop shadows tinted with an earthen umber cast rather than generic pitch black:
   - *Floating Card / Dropdown:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 9, 8, 0.65), 0 0 0 1px #3f3a34`
   - *Modal Overlay:* `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(8, 7, 6, 0.85), 0 0 0 1px #4a433b`
   - *Active / Focus State:* Subtle terracotta bloom via `0 0 0 3px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design uses an inviting, modern curvature that softens the dense, structured nature of educational management systems:

- **Standard Elements (roundedness: 2 / 0.5rem [8px]):** Applied to inputs, buttons, list selections, and inline tags.
- **Cards & Content Panels (`rounded-xl` / 1rem [16px]):** Standard for academic module cards, assignment containers, and metric tiles.
- **Modal Dialogs & Shells (`rounded-2xl` / 1.5rem [24px]):** Used on modal viewports, floating slide-overs, and primary dashboard hero surfaces.
- **Pill Badges (`rounded-full`):** Reserved strictly for numeric student counts, grading statuses, and active course indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#ea580c` with text in `#f5f3f0` (`label-lg`, semibold). On hover, shifts to `#f97316`. Active states settle on `#c2410c`. Border radius is 8px (`rounded-md`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background in `#24211e`, border in `1px solid #3f3a34`, text in `#f5f3f0`. Hover transitions background to `#2e2a26` with border highlighted to `#fb923c`.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text in `#e2ded9`. On hover, fills with `rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.08)` and text turns `#fb923c`.

### Input Fields
- **Default State:** Background in `#1a1816`, perimeter border `1px solid #3f3a34`, text in `#f5f3f0`, placeholder in `#a8a29e`. Internal padding of `space-sm` vertical, `space-md` horizontal.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#ea580c` with a synchronized focus ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.2)`.

### Cards & Content Containers
- **Resting:** Background `#24211e`, border `1px solid #3f3a34`, corner radius `rounded-xl` (16px), inner padding `space-lg` (24px).
- **Interactive Hover:** Card border warms to `#fb923c`, surface lightens slightly to `#2e2a26`, and subtle elevation shadow deepens.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Pill-shaped or 8px rounded corners. Neutral tags use background `#2e2a26`, text `#e2ded9`.
- **Terracotta Accent Chips:** Background `rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.15)`, text `#fb923c`, border `1px solid rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.3)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkbox:** 18x18px box with 4px border radius. Unchecked: `#1a1816` background, `#3f3a34` border. Checked: `#ea580c` background with `#f5f3f0` checkmark icon.
- **Radio:** 18x18px circular element. Unchecked: `#1a1816` background, `#3f3a34` border. Checked: `#ea580c` ring with concentric 6px dot in `#f5f3f0`.

### Lists & Tables
- **Data Rows:** Background alternating between transparent and `#1a1816`. Subtle border bottom of `1px solid #2e2a26`.
- **Selected Row:** Surface fills with `rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.08)`, left edge accented by a 3px vertical bar in `#ea580c`.
- **Header Row:** Text in `label-sm` uppercase, color `#a8a29e`, background `#1a1816`.

### Academic-Specific Additions
- **Syllabus / Lecture Accordion:** Contained inside `#24211e` panels with `#3f3a34` dividers. Active module displays an indicator dot in `#ea580c`.
- **Grade Pill / Score Indicator:** Pill badge rendered in clay tones: High Distinction (`#ea580c` tint), In Progress (`rgba(251, 146, 60, 0.15)`), and Complete (`#2e2a26` with `#e2ded9` text).
