## Brand & Style

This design system pairs grounded academic utility with physical, artisanal warmth. Built for modern educators, students, and institutional leaders, the interface steps away from sterile corporate software and utilitarian dashboards in favor of an organic, human-centered study environment. It evokes feelings of mindful organization, tactile focus, architectural permanence, and scholarly calm.

The visual aesthetic synthesizes modern minimalism with organic tactile surfaces:
- **Tonal Organic Warmth:** Rich, earthbound neutrals mimic natural paper, fine clay pottery, and linen study surfaces rather than artificial stark white pixels.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Information is framed with purposeful structural borders, balanced whitespace, and crisp typographical hierarchy.
- **Warm Tactility:** Surfaces rely on soft clay-tinted ambient drops, rounded card contours, and subtle tactile interactive states that feel grounded under hand and eye.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from earthen materials—sun-baked terra cotta, riverbed slate teal, unglazed earthenware, and parchment.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#c2410c` - Terracotta Rust):** Anchors core actions, key active states, brand signifiers, and high-priority milestones.
- **Secondary (`#0f766e` - Slate Teal):** Represents positive growth, completed coursework, verified attendance, and balanced academic progress.
- **Tertiary (`#9a3412` - Muted Deep Clay):** Used for structural accents, focus rings, and high-emphasis contextual cues.
- **Warning & Highlight (`#b45309` - Terracotta Amber):** Alerts users to pending deadlines, schedule conflicts, and actionable review notices.
- **Critical Alert (`#ea580c` / `#991b1b` - Sunken Clay Red):** Reserved for overdue submissions, missing prerequisites, or urgent systemic warnings.

### Surface & Neutral Tiers
- **Base Canvas (`#fdfbf7`):** Warm alabaster parchment.
- **Card / Surface Default (`#ffffff`):** Pure contrast container surface sitting on top of canvas.
- **Muted Surface / Inset (`#f7f4ee`):** Subtle secondary container or table header band.
- **Recessed Surface (`#eee9df`):** Active toggles, chip backdrops, and unselected structural panels.
- **Border Default (`#e7dfd5`):** Warm stone delineation providing structure without high-contrast severity.
- **Border Strong (`#d6cbbe`):** Focused inputs, interactive borders, and key dividing lines.

### Text & Contrast
- **Headline / Title (`#292524` - Espresso Charcoal):** Maximum legibility for academic reading.
- **Body Default (`#44403c` - Smoked Umber):** Comfortable, softened reading contrast for sustained attention.
- **Muted / Subtitle (`#78716c` - Muted Clay Stone):** Captions, secondary timestamps, and supportive metadata.

## Typography

Typographic scale is powered exclusively by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its crisp geometric legibility blended with open, humanist counters.

### Application Guidelines
- **Academic Hierarchy:** Major gradebook and curriculum titles use tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) and firm font weights (`700` and `800`) to anchor structure.
- **Reading Comfort:** Body copy maintains a generous `1.5` to `1.55` proportional line height in regular (`400`) weight, ensuring high stamina during extended grading, assessment, or syllabus review sessions.
- **System Labels & Badges:** Micro-labels, badge tags, and tabular data labels leverage uppercase or small-caps letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) in semibold (`600` or `700`) to maximize scan efficiency.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an adaptable 12-column fluid grid system with purposeful visual margins and rhythmic vertical padding based on an 8-point base module.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, `2rem` (`32px`) to `3rem` (`48px`) margins. Sidebar controls occupy 3 columns (or a fixed 280px drawer), with central instructional feeds spanning 9 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters, `1.5rem` (`24px`) outer margin. Secondary side panels collapse to full-width horizontal tabs or slide-over overlays.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `1rem` (`16px`) outer margins. Multi-column grading matrices gracefully collapse into stacked sequential cards.

### Spacing Rhythm
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-spacing between paired icons and adjacent labels.
- `space-sm` (8px): Internal button horizontal offsets, chip internal spacing, and stack gap within list items.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard gap between form fields, module rows, and card inner contents.
- `space-lg` (24px): Padding inside classroom dashboard cards and gap between distinct widget groups.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section-to-section layout spacing dividing distinct functional workflows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use cold, synthetic grayscale drop shadows. Instead, it employs **Warm Ambient Clay Shadows** tinted with deep umber and terracotta tones (`rgba(41, 37, 36, ...)`), producing an organic, sunlit paper feel.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Recessed):** Background canvas (`#fdfbf7`) or inset containers (`#f7f4ee`) with no shadow. Surrounded by a 1px solid warm stone border (`#e7dfd5`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Background `#ffffff`, subtle 1px border (`#e7dfd5`), shadow: `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(68, 64, 60, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Card & Floating Toolbars):** Slight vertical lift with deepened warm aura: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(68, 64, 60, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Popovers):** Prominent focal point above backdrop: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.14), 0 6px 16px -3px rgba(154, 52, 18, 0.08)`. Backdrops use `rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.4)` paired with an ultra-light 2px blur.

## Shapes

The design uses **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry to reinforce the approachable, soft-hewn aesthetic of shaped clay and modern architectural stationery.

### Geometric Rules
- **Micro Elements (Pills, Chips, Avatars):** Full circular roundness (`rounded-full` or `9999px`) to create smooth touch targets.
- **Controls & Form Elements:** `0.5rem` (`8px`) for standard inputs, text areas, dropdown triggers, and buttons.
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** `1rem` (`16px`) to `1.5rem` (`24px`) (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`) for schedule cards, student performance summaries, and submission tiles.
- **Floating Modals & Large Canvas Drawers:** `1.5rem` (`24px`) rounded corners to keep large containers friendly and organic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Terracotta Rust background (`#c2410c`), text `#ffffff`, font weight `600`, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`, radius `0.5rem`. Hover state transitions to deep rust (`#9a3412`) with Level 2 warm shadow. Active state scales down subtly (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary (Slate Teal):** Slate Teal background (`#0f766e`), text `#ffffff`, used for submission approvals, attendance confirmations, and forward progress.
- **Outline / Neutral:** Background `#ffffff`, border `1.5px solid #e7dfd5`, text `#44403c`. On hover, background shifts to `#f7f4ee` and border tint warms to `#d6cbbe`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#44403c`. On hover, background shifts to `#eee9df`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Height `24px` to `28px`, radius `9999px`, font `label-md`.
  - *On Track / Passed:* Slate teal tint background (`#0f766e` at 10% opacity) with text `#0f766e`.
  - *Warning / Due Soon:* Amber tint background (`#b45309` at 12% opacity) with text `#b45309`.
  - *Alert / Needs Action:* Terracotta red tint background (`#ea580c` at 12% opacity) with text `#c2410c`.
  - *Informational / Category:* Sand tint background (`#eee9df`) with text `#44403c`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#ffffff`, border `1.5px solid #e7dfd5`, padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`, text `#292524`, placeholder `#78716c`.
- **Focus State:** Border changes to Terracotta Rust (`#c2410c`) accompanied by a subtle 3px outer ring: `rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.15)`.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Size `20px x 20px`, border `2px solid #d6cbbe`, radius `6px` (checkbox) or `50%` (radio). When checked, filled with Terracotta Rust (`#c2410c`) and displaying a crisp white indicator.

### Cards & Curriculum Modules
- **Default Card:** Surface `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e7dfd5`, corner radius `1rem` (`16px`), padding `1.5rem`.
- **Header Section:** Bottom divider in `#f7f4ee` separating course heading from dynamic metrics.
- **Interactive Card:** When actionable, adds smooth translation (`translateY(-2px)`) and elevates to Level 2 warm shadow on hover.

### Specialized Education Components
- **Grade Progress Gauge:** Slate Teal (`#0f766e`) track fill with soft sand (`#eee9df`) empty track.
- **Student Roster Row:** Interactive list items with subtle `#fdfbf7` hover background, separated by `1px solid #f7f4ee`, featuring round student avatars bordered with `#e7dfd5`.
- **Assignment Timeline Rail:** Vertical thread styled with 2px solid `#e7dfd5`, using Terracotta and Slate Teal circular milestone nodes.
